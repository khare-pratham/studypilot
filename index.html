Add your own study notes, search for concepts, and ask questions. StudyPilot retrieves relevant passages and shows where they came from instead of inventing answers.

Prototype scope: this version uses local keyword retrieval and extractive answers in your browser. It does not call an AI model or upload your notes. It supports text, Markdown and text-based CSV files; PDF parsing and LLM integration are future extensions.
